Tabulka deklinací Slunce

The declination is the Sun's angle north or south of the celestial equator.

+ the Sun is above (north of) the celestial equator · − below (south of) it.

Values change by a few arcminutes from year to year. Click any cell to look up that day. The noon height and day length are calculated from the table value and your latitude (day length ignores refraction, so real days are a few minutes longer).
